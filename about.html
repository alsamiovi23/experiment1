Who is this guy?

The slightly longer version of the homepage answer.

I'm eighteen, based in Bangladesh, and still working out exactly what I want to specialize in.

So far, that uncertainty has taken the shape of debate, research, academic competitions, books, random internet rabbit holes, and occasionally spending three hours researching something that absolutely did not need three hours of research.

I like psychology, literature, technology, OSINT, games, films, and questions that don't have particularly convenient answers.

I don't really know where all of this is going yet. That's part of the fun.
