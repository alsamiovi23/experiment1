OSINT.

The digital Sherlock Holmes side of things: finding, connecting, verifying, and understanding publicly available information.

Why OSINT interests me

Open-source intelligence, digital investigation, search techniques, metadata, digital footprints, and ethical investigative methodology — the appeal is basically research with a puzzle attached.

A note on ethics

Everything here is meant to be educational. OSINT is about understanding publicly available information responsibly — not about tracking, harassing, or invading anyone's privacy.
